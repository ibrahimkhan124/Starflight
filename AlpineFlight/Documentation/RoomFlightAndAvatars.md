# Room flight and pilot profiles

Open My creations → Open room flight on a supported iPhone or iPad. Allow Camera access, slowly scan a well-lit floor or table, then aim the centre crosshair at the detected surface and choose Place ship. The selected ship or imported model is miniaturised. Steer with the joystick; pull down to climb, and use signed thrust to move forward or backward. Stop / hover sets thrust to zero. Remain stationary with a clear space. Real furniture does not have collision geometry.

Choose Gate, Tower or Asteroid in the Add menu and tap Place object to place it on the scanned surface. Undo removes the last object; Reset scan clears the temporary course. There is a 20-object limit and a 3 m boundary from the camera. The course is session-only, not a saved AR world map. Tracking loss and backgrounding stop flight; Pause and Done stop thrust. Camera images are not recorded or uploaded. Mac and simulator world tracking are unavailable; the view provides an explanation.

Open Pilot logbook → Pilot profile & avatar, or Shop → Pilot profile & avatar outfitter. Name the pilot, choose Human, Droid or Alien, pick a suit colour, and buy/equip one accessory with existing credits. Six accessories are available. The daily wheel has a new accessory sector; it awards an unowned item, with 250 credits as a fallback when all are owned. The existing daily limit still applies. Profile data is optional in the existing Codable wallet for backward compatibility and follows that wallet through the existing account save system. Badges now have pictorial medal artwork and explicit locked labels.

AR camera behaviour, tracking stability and the physical scale must be tested on a real device. Compilation and simulator checks do not establish room tracking performance.
