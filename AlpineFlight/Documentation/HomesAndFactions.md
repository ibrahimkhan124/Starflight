# Coruscant homes, contacts and planet comets

Version 1.13, build 19. All prices use earned game credits. No account, new permission or real-money purchase is required.

Each plot clears a 1.5 km square, including conflicting tower/tunnel geometry. Purchases show a confirmation with the full price and build the selected residence immediately. Gardens, thin light strips and water are decorative.

The city chart marks Estates 1–4 in purple. **Fly to this residence** transfers you to the clear front avenue at 260 m, facing the home. This is available only on Coruscant with no active city contract or bounty; transfers reset the flight tracker’s position sample, so they do not earn distance or low-flight points.

## Furniture interactions and outdoor editing

After entering, use **Explore → Terrace** to visit your private outdoor space; **Explore → Inside** returns to the room. The ship stays parked, and changing area earns no flight distance or credits. Both areas use the walking controls and **Arrange room / Arrange terrace**. Outdoor arrangements also appear beside the actual house during city flight and in its exterior preview. The architecture itself remains fixed; this editor changes its furnished terrace. Outdoor furnishings are decorative to aircraft and cannot obstruct the checked landing route.

Approach a furnishing within 2.6 m of its footprint. Its contextual action appears at the upper left; tap it or press **E** with a keyboard. Seating switches to a seated viewpoint; **Stand up** or movement returns you to your safe standing position. The display cabinet's doors slide open, lamps and canopy switch light on/off, and the fountain's jets and fire pit can be stopped. The holo table and desk cycle three coloured planetary displays. Watering a garden reveals blossoms. Paintings open an enlarged inspection sheet; sculptures, trophies and the aquarium have collection descriptions.

The sound console plays an original eight-second electronic lounge loop. It obeys Sound and master volume, and stops when the room is hidden, when visiting the terrace, when leaving home or when the app backgrounds. Interaction settings persist per home, including after removing and reinstalling a furnishing. They do not charge or reward currency.

Paintings slide along the left gallery wall and cannot rotate onto the floor. Pendants occupy the ceiling layer and can hang above floor furniture. Indoor and outdoor footprints are independent. The terrace has a 40 × 32 m arrangement grid mapped at three city-world units per metre beside the existing landing apron. Its railings, furniture positions, rotations and colours share geometry between the walkable view, preview and batched RealityKit display; no terrain or city reload is required for an edit.

## Land and enter

1. Buy a home, enter Coruscant, then open **Flight tools → Homes & interior shop → Fly to this residence**. You can also fly there yourself; the four estates are marked on the city map.
2. Within 1.4 km, the HUD shows your residence and its terrace bearing/distance. Inbound approach assist slows you within 1.1 km, and **Land at home** becomes available within 750 m when no city job or bounty is active.
3. Landing validates the complete route against actual city and owned-home geometry. Repulsor brakes stop the ship, climb to a safe height, align above the terrace and descend smoothly. The route accommodates every ship, including the large pods and capital-style craft. A blocked route is rejected with a clear approach message.
4. Once parked, use **Enter home** for a full-screen room. Flight, fuel travel and comet damage pause while you are inside; the parked ship does not drift. You can walk with **WASD / arrow keys** or the touch joystick and drag the room view to look around. **Walk / Brisk walk** switches between 3 and 5.5 m/s. Movement eases into motion, brakes promptly when released, and stops immediately when leaving the room or backgrounding. Walls and installed furnishings block walking, including while moving briskly.
5. **Return to ship** puts you back at the same terrace. It does not launch the ship. **Depart** performs a smooth vertical lift followed by an outward flight along the cleared avenue. Steering resumes after the departure manoeuvre. Outbound flights are not slowed by approach assist.

Other jobs must be finished or cancelled before landing at home. Depart before accepting new work. Restarting, returning to base or changing planet clears the temporary parked/inside state. Home ownership, furnishings and room layouts persist.

## Arrange furniture

Every residence has a **40 × 32 m room (1,280 m²)** with an 8 m ceiling, four times the previous floor area. The panoramic window, skyline, lighting and shop camera fit the larger space. Walking bounds and the floor plan match the visible room; the entrance stays clear. Existing furniture keeps its original size and saved position, so your arrangements are preserved. You can move it out into the new floor space without paying again.

The walkable room has a closed ceiling, entrance wall and side glazing. The shop and editor use a cutaway of the same room so you can inspect the furniture without walls hiding it.

Use **Arrange room** while inside, or open it from an owned home's shop page. Select an installed furniture set and drag its footprint on the floor plan. The numbered footprints match the furniture list and retain their actual relative sizes. Grid lines are 2 m apart; **Snap to half-metre grid** rounds a dragged position to 0.5 m and can be turned off. Movement buttons shift it by half a metre; **Rotate 90°** turns it. A red footprint and live explanation identify an overlapping furniture set, a wall or the entrance. Valid changes save immediately and cost no credits. Positions are independent for each house.

**Undo / Redo** restores up to 40 changes during the current editor visit, including rotations and resets. A new edit clears the redo branch. Only room placement changes enter the history: it never reverses a purchase, alters credits or reinstalls an item. Restoring a layout validates the whole arrangement before saving it once. If furnishings changed, the stale history is rejected. The history itself clears when leaving the editor; the latest saved layout persists. The 3D preview keeps its camera angle when a furnishing is moved or rotated.

The 3D preview, first-person room and walking collision all use the saved position and rotation. **Reset furniture positions** restores the original arrangement without removing purchased items. Removing a furnishing stores it; reinstalling restores its saved position when clear, or finds nearby free space. If the room cannot fit the item, it stays stored. Ceiling lighting is fixed to the room and cannot be dragged.

## Republic, Empire and bounty work

On Coruscant, open **Flight tools → Contacts & bounties**. Choose who to work for per assignment. There is no permanent side lock; Republic, Imperial and Guild standing are independent.

| Side | Assignment | Base reward | Possible bonus |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Republic | Relief Convoy: Medical → Market | 130 | 15 |
| Republic | Civilian Evacuation: Industrial → Medical | 170 | 20 |
| Empire | Classified Dispatch: Senate → Industrial | 150 | 15 |
| Empire | Surveillance Sweep: Spaceport → Senate → Industrial | 140 | 20 |
| Guild | The Neon Runner: disable one pirate | 180 | — |
| Guild | Syndicate Wing: disable three pirates | 320 | — |

Every completed assignment adds ten standing with its side. Guild standing of 20 unlocks Syndicate Wing. Side ranks advance at 10, 40 and 100 standing. Station contracts use the existing docking, condition ratings, deadlines and saved city career debriefs. Transport deadlines are nine minutes; the surveillance sweep is untimed and each scan takes eight seconds.

Bounty targets orbit above the towers at approximately 1,000 m. Follow the HUD bearing, distance and orange target shield. Use your existing **Fire** control to disable 85 shield points per target; ship-specific gun damage applies. Targets return 110 m/s shots no more than once every 5.5 seconds, dealing three shield damage per hit. Complete all targets to collect the reward. Each assignment carries a receipt so it cannot be paid twice. Only one city contract or bounty can be active. Cancelling, crashing, leaving Coruscant or restarting ends unfinished work without payment. Completed rewards and standing persist; active hunts do not resume after quitting.

## Planet-only comets

Comets now appear only while exploring Coruscant, Tatooine, Hoth or Endor. They are hidden in orbit, asteroid challenges, normal mountain regions and hyperspace. A prepared pool uses icy white nuclei, translucent coma and four fading tail bands. Full detail shows up to three comets; reduced detail shows up to two.

Most passes cross overhead safely. Every third pass can cross your predicted flight corridor, with **COMET CROSSING · WATCH THE SKY** displayed before arrival. Changing speed or direction can avoid the crossing. Only the head causes damage; the tail does not. A swept relative collision check handles fast movement. A strike removes twelve shield points, plays shield impact feedback and consumes that comet. It can only crash a ship whose shields were already low. Flight pauses also pause comets. Leaving a planet clears its comet field.
