# Free-roam activity prototype

Every free-roam flight begins at the central rendezvous, 400 metres above terrain, heading toward Open Skies. Four fixed sectors occupy the corners of the prepared 9.6 km region:

- Southwest: Open Skies, for unrestricted flying.
- Northwest: Hyperspace Port, with the existing four asteroid destinations and five-second jumps.
- Southeast: Landing Academy, with a lit practice pad.
- Northeast: Mission Outpost, with a second pad and assignments.

Open NAV COMPUTER in the HUD and choose Go to an area to transfer directly there. Transfers reset weapons and controls, cancel any active mission, and place the aircraft safely above terrain. Landing Academy arrives aligned with the runway, with approach assist enabled and throttle set to 28%. Fly within 900 metres of the outpost to accept Ridge Survey, Supply Delivery, or Fighter Interception. Survey requires three beacon passes. Delivery requires three passes followed by a safe landing at the academy. Fighter Interception has three visible TIE fighters with 24 health each. Player shots use swept collision and each ship's weapon damage. Fighters patrol ahead and fire slow, infrequent bolts that deal four shield damage; eliminating all three completes the mission. Missions can be cancelled and repeated without ending free roam. Completion and landing counts last for this flight.

Near a pad, Approach Assist provides optional hover-style controls for all craft. Throttle sets horizontal speed from 0 to 65 m/s; pitch commands climb/descent up to 12 m/s; roll and yaw turn the ship. Reduce throttle below roughly 33%, descend gently, and level the ship before touchdown. Safe landings require horizontal speed at most 22 m/s, descent at most 6 m/s, and pitch/roll within about 11 degrees. A fast or steep touchdown is a crash. Take Off lifts the ship clear and returns to ordinary flight physics. Pausing freezes the simulation.

The two pads sit on flattened terrain generated by the same height function used by collision. Their physical footprint is 440 by 1,700 metres. Flat clearings extend 1,600 metres in both directions from each strip's centre, with smooth terrain transitions beyond. Touchdown uses a swept intersection and uses the aircraft collision probes for underside clearance and requires margin for the body radius. Scenery is built once, with reused materials; one beacon moves as the mission progresses. No new terrain streaming is introduced.

Cockpit systems now show live engine throttle, shields, vertical speed and flight/landing status. Neighboring-planet hyperspace travel and hangar narration are deferred for a later design discussion.

The two buttons above throttle are labelled FIRE and CAMERA. FIRE toggles continuous shooting, with an active colour and accessibility state; tap it again to stop. CAMERA switches chase/cockpit views. Pod boost and skid controls remain beside the joystick, so the weapons row still has two buttons. Pods retain hover assist after a runway takeoff to avoid ground-racing physics snapping them below the platform.

Hangar zoom uses the visible plus/minus magnifier buttons and Reset View. The camera eases toward a bounded distance between 18 and 44 metres; Reduce Motion applies the distance directly. The paint selector has been removed from Settings. The creator calls its deterministic seed Landscape Variation and explains that repeating the number reproduces the terrain.
